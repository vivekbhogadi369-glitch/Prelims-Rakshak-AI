Job Alerts

What are Government Job Alerts?

Government job alerts help students track official recruitment notifications, application dates, eligibility, syllabus, exam pattern, admit cards, results and important updates related to different government exams in India.

Major Government Job Categories

  • UPSC: Civil Services, CAPF, CDS, NDA and other central services
  • State PSC: Group 1, Group 2, State Civil Services and departmental posts
  • SSC: CGL, CHSL, MTS, CPO, GD Constable and related posts
  • Banking: IBPS, SBI, RBI, NABARD and insurance sector exams
  • Railways: RRB NTPC, Group D, ALP, Technician and other railway posts
  • Defence: Army, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard and paramilitary forces
  • Teaching: TET, CTET, DSC, KVS, NVS and other teaching posts
  • PSUs: Public Sector Undertaking jobs through GATE and direct recruitment

What should students check?

  • Notification release date
  • Last date to apply
  • Eligibility criteria
  • Age limit and relaxations
  • Number of vacancies
  • Exam pattern and syllabus
  • Admit card, exam date and result updates

How to use Job Alerts smartly?

  • Do not apply blindly for every exam.
  • Choose exams based on your qualification, age, interest and preparation level.
  • Maintain a small notebook or digital list of exams you are eligible for.
  • Check notifications regularly so that you do not miss deadlines.
  • Always verify important details from the official notification before applying.
⚠️ Never depend only on social media posts. Always verify exam details from the official notification.
