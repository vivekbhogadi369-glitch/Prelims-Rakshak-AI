What is PIB?

PIB stands for Press Information Bureau. It is the official communication agency of the Government of India. It publishes official updates about government policies, schemes, programmes, reports, ministries, and important national developments.

Why is PIB important for government exams?

PIB is very useful for UPSC, State PSC, SSC, Banking, Railways and other government exams because many current affairs questions are connected to government schemes, ministries, policy decisions, reports, indexes and official initiatives.

What should students focus on?

  • Government schemes and their ministries
  • New policies, missions and programmes
  • Reports, indexes and rankings
  • Environment, science and technology updates
  • Economy, agriculture and social sector initiatives
  • International agreements involving India
